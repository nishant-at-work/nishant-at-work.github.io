  I am a Fellow at [IIHS (deemed to be) University](https://iihs.co.in), working on urban transport systems, with keen interest in issues of safety, accessibility, affordability, inequality and financing of infrastructure. I also coordinate the [Public Transport Forum](https://ptf.neocities.org), a citizen collective working on bus transport across Indian cities. Most of what I care about sits somewhere between research, policy, and people organising together.
