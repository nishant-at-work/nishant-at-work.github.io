Organised as part of my work at People's Resource Centre, across two editions in 2020 and 2021.

The series brought together urban farmers, fishers, pastoralists, researchers, planners and civil society groups to treat agriculture not as a rural subject that strays into cities, but as an urban practice with its own claims on land, water and policy attention. Sessions covered tenure on floodplains, livelihoods on common land, river ecologies, and the absence of agriculture from master planning.
