Organised across two editions, in collaboration with the Institute for Democracy and Sustainability and the Transportation Research and Injury Prevention Programme at IIT Delhi.

The first edition ran over six days in April 2020. Planned as an in-person gathering and moved online days before it opened, it drew more than 550 participants — academics, activists, urban farming practitioners, researchers and students — across sessions with over fifteen speakers, with volunteer translation to widen access. A second edition followed in 2021.

The convention treated agriculture not as a rural subject that strays into cities, but as an urban practice with its own claims on land, water and policy attention. Sessions ran across tenure on floodplains, livelihoods on common land, river ecologies, farm labour, and the absence of agriculture from master planning. Proceedings were published as a report in English and Hindi, and the recordings made publicly available.
